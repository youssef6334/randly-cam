Randly Rules

Last updated: October 4, 2026

By using Randly you agree to follow these rules. Breaking them may result in a temporary ban from the service.

1. Minimum age

Randly may only be used by people aged 18 or older. We ask you to confirm this the first time you use the site, and by continuing you confirm that your confirmation is true.

2. Prohibited content

  • Showing or sharing nude or sexual content
  • Violence, incitement to hatred, or racial, religious or sexual discrimination
  • Sharing sensitive personal information (phone numbers, addresses, financial details) about yourself or others
  • Commercial advertising or promoting other sites and services
  • Harassment, bullying or threats of any kind
  • Any content involving the exploitation or harm of minors

3. Reporting and enforcement

If you encounter behavior that breaks these rules, use the Report button 🚩 during the chat. We log the report with its reason and a hashed identifier of the IP address; we do not keep the content of the conversation. When a user is reported by several different people within a short period, they are temporarily blocked automatically.

Protection currently relies on user reports and automatic temporary bans. There is no automated scanning of video or text content.

4. Limits of our responsibility

Randly connects strangers at random. We do our best to provide a respectful environment, but we cannot guarantee the behavior of every user, so chat with care and never share private information.

5. Contact us

For questions about these rules, write to us at: contact@randly.live
