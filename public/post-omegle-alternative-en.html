Why did Omegle shut down?

In November 2023, Omegle's founder, Leif K-Brooks, announced the permanent shutdown of the site after years of pressure over misuse of the platform by some users, especially regarding minors' safety. That decision left a real gap in the "random chat" market and pushed users to look for alternatives that keep the same freedom with stronger safety.
