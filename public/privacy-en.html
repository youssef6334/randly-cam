Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we process when you use Randly and how we handle it.

2. Data we process

  • Randly does not require an account or any personal registration.
  • We use your IP address at connection time to estimate your approximate country (to improve matching) and to limit usage and prevent abuse. We do not store the IP address itself; we keep only a hashed identifier of it when a report is filed.
  • Our hosting infrastructure (Microsoft Azure) may keep standard technical logs that include IP addresses.
  • Your browser stores your preferences only (localStorage): language, dark/light theme, sound mute and age confirmation.

3. Video and text chat

Video calls travel directly between the two users using WebRTC (and may pass through a relay server when needed); we do not record them. Text messages pass through our server only to be delivered to the other person and are not stored. When a report is filed we keep only its reason and the hashed identifier, not the conversation.

Technical note: in a direct video connection, your IP address may be visible to the other person.

4. Instant translation

If you turn on instant translation, the text of the messages you receive is sent through our server to a third-party translation service (Google) to be translated. We do not store it. The feature is optional: keep the "No translation" option to turn it off.

5. Advertising and cookies

The site shows ads through Google AdSense and other ad networks, which may use cookies and similar identifiers to show and measure ads. You can manage personalized Google ads in Ad Settings, or delete cookies in your browser settings.

6. Age

The service is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Contact us

For privacy questions, write to us at: contact@randly.live
