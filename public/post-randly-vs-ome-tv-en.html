Randly vs Ome.tv: Which Random Video Chat Is Better in 2026?

Last updated: October 2026

Ome.tv is one of the best-known names in random video chat, and Randly is a newer free alternative. If you are deciding between them, this comparison covers the things that matter most: access, features, safety and mobile use.

We tried both sites ourselves and wrote down what we found. Everything we say about Ome.tv below comes from our own testing, so if the site changes something later, the details may change too. Here is how they compare, step by step.

How We Compared Them

We looked at both sites the way a normal first-time user would. We asked simple questions:

  • Can I start chatting without creating an account?
  • Which features are free, and which cost money?
  • Can I choose a country?
  • Is there live translation?
  • Is it video only, or can I also use text chat?
  • Does it work in a browser, and is an app required?
  • Is there a way to report and ban bad users?
  • Does the site make it clear that it is for adults only?

These are the same questions most people have before they pick a random chat site, so we used them to build the comparison below.

Access and Price

Randly needs no account. You open the site, choose video or text, and press Start. In our testing, Ome.tv required us to register before chatting, and that was true even after downloading their app. With Randly you can stream and chat as a guest, with no account at all.

This matters more than it sounds. Registration means choosing a username, entering details and waiting, and many people simply close the tab at that point. If you want to try random chat for five minutes, a site that lets you start immediately is a much better fit.

On price, Randly has no paid plan at all. Every feature is available to everyone. In our testing, Ome.tv's basic chat was free, but filters and translation were paid features.

Matching and Filters

Randly gives you a global mode to meet anyone, or a country filter if you want to talk to people from a specific place. You can also add interests so you are matched with people who share them, something we did not find on Ome.tv.

This is one of the biggest differences. Say you are from Egypt and want to talk to someone from Turkey: on Randly, picking a country is free. In our testing, Ome.tv charged for country filtering.

Interest matching also changes how the chats feel. When you are matched with someone who likes the same games, music or topics, you do not need to invent a conversation from nothing. You already have something to talk about.

Video and Text Chat

Randly offers both video chat and text chat. In our testing, Ome.tv was video only, with no text chat option.

Having both options is useful in practice. Sometimes you are in a place where you cannot use your camera or microphone, or you simply prefer typing, especially when you are practicing a language. With text chat available, you can still use the site in those moments.

Language and Translation

If you want to chat across languages, Randly offers optional live translation for text chat in 12 languages. On Randly, an Egyptian user can chat with a Turkish user and read the conversation in Arabic at no cost. In our testing, Ome.tv put translation behind a paid plan.

For anyone who uses random chat to practice a language or meet people from other cultures, this difference is significant. Free translation turns a "hello" and nothing else into a conversation you can really follow.

Mobile Experience

Randly works directly in your phone's browser, with no app required. You open the link and you are in. Ome.tv works in a browser and also has an app, and in our testing both options required registration.

If you mostly chat from your phone, the simplest way is the one that does not ask you to install anything or create an account first.

Safety and Moderation

Both sites offer reporting and banning. Randly includes an instant report option and automatically bans users who receive multiple reports, for a limited time. Randly also does not store your conversations, and the first screen asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before you can enter. Under-18s are not allowed. In our testing, Ome.tv did not show us an age notice when we entered.

Whichever site you pick, the same rules apply: never share personal details, skip anyone who makes you uncomfortable, and remember you must be 18 or older.

Here is how the difference felt when we tried both. On Ome.tv, we had to register before we could start, on the website and again in the app. On Randly, we confirmed our age and started chatting right away as guests. Then, when we wanted to talk to someone from a specific country, Randly let us choose one for free, while Ome.tv asked for payment. Live translation worked the same way: free on Randly, paid on Ome.tv.

Which One Fits Which Situation?

The best choice depends on what you want from a random chat. Here is a simple way to think about it:

  • You want to try it right now, with no setup. Randly is the better fit, because you can start as a guest without an account.
  • You want to talk to people from one specific country. Randly lets you choose a country for free, while in our testing Ome.tv charged for this.
  • You want to practice a language. Free live translation in 12 languages, plus text chat, makes Randly easier for language practice.
  • You want to chat by typing instead of using your camera. Randly has text chat. In our testing, Ome.tv was video only.
  • You are already used to Ome.tv and do not mind registering. Then you may simply prefer to stay with what you know.

Tips for Better Chats on Either Site

A few habits improve the experience on any random chat site:

  • Start with a real question, not just "hi". Something like "what are you into these days?" gets a better answer.
  • Use filters when you have them. A country or interest match saves you from skipping again and again.
  • Do not take a rude person personally. Skip them, report them if they break the rules, and move on.
  • Keep your personal details private, even if the conversation feels friendly.

Safety Tips for Any Random Chat Site

No matter which site you use, a few habits keep you safer:

  1. Never share your full name, address, school, workplace or phone number.
  2. Do not click links from strangers.
  3. Skip or report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
  4. Remember that anyone can record their screen.
  5. Do not send money or gifts to people you meet in random chats.
