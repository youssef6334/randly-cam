About Randly: Free Random Video and Text Chat Without Sign-Up

Randly is a free website for random video and text chat with people around the world. There is nothing to install and no account to create: open the site, choose Video Chat or Text Chat, and you are connected to someone who is online right now.

What you can do on Randly

  • Random video chat and text chat with strangers, free of charge.
  • Interest matching: add topics such as football, gaming or coding and Randly tries to connect you with someone who shares them.
  • Country selection: choose a country, or stay on Global to match with anyone.
  • Instant translation of text messages into 12 languages (see the Privacy Policy for how it works).
  • Private rooms: invite a friend with a link that stays valid for 72 hours.

Safety and privacy

Randly is for adults aged 18 and over. You can report any user with one tap, and users who receive reports from several different people are automatically blocked for a short time. We do not record or store your conversations, and no account or personal registration is required.

No moderation system catches everything, so never share details such as your phone number, address or financial information with strangers. Please read our Rules, Privacy Policy and Terms.
